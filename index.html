Questions answered

Frequently asked questions.

Some helpful details before you start your project.

What is included in the ₹15,000 plan?
The plan covers website development and domain/hosting setup within the agreed scope. After handover, you manage the website yourself. Confirm the domain and hosting ownership, renewal costs, included pages and any third-party charges before starting.
What does the ₹5,000 monthly plan include?
It includes the agreed website setup, domain and hosting, and ongoing website management. The monthly scope, update limits, response times and exclusions should be confirmed in your service agreement.
Will I get access to my website?
Yes. The managed plan is intended to provide client admin access, while runfourcode retains super-admin access for management. Exact permissions depend on the website platform and agreed setup.
Will my website automatically collect leads?
A website can collect enquiries when a working contact form and the necessary email delivery, database or CRM integration are configured and tested. Lead capture is not guaranteed by hosting alone. We can agree on the appropriate setup for your project.
Can I request software, CRM or marketing services?
Yes. Use the enquiry dashboard to describe what you need. Software, CRM, automation, social media and marketing projects can be discussed separately and quoted according to their requirements.
